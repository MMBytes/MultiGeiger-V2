## Overall verdict

This changeset mostly meets the project's house-style bar, and does well on the primary structural conventions: safe string-building helpers are used consistently, the `HAL_HAS_FUEL_GAUGE` stub pattern exactly follows the `als.c` template, the helper-function convention is respected, and the `fuel_gauge_ready()`→`fuel_gauge_present()` rename that spanned this range left no dangling references in the code. However, the review surfaced a real documentation-hygiene gap around the multi-commit iteration this feature went through: two board/HAL-layer comments (`hal.h:38`, `mqtt_discovery.c:167`) still describe the discarded VCELL-auto-detect design and will mislead future readers about how battery-presence gating actually works; one stray version tag (`i2c_bus.h:106`, "V2.6.7") doesn't match the actual `VERSION_STR`; and the final CHANGELOG rewrite (`86a0273`) retained a reference to `reg_read8()`, a helper that had already been deleted two commits earlier. All three are drive-by consequences of consolidating a fast-iterating six-commit feature arc into one final state/changelog without a last-pass grep for now-stale prose — worth a quick follow-up commit to fix, but none of them affect firmware behavior.
